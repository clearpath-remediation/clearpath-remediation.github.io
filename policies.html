Refund and cancellation policy

  • Before work starts: cancel any order for a full refund.
  • After work starts: we refund pages we have not yet remediated. Completed pages are delivered and billed.
  • Accuracy guarantee: if a delivered page fails a PAC or Acrobat accessibility check, or a WCAG 2.1 AA issue we were responsible for, tell us within 30 days and we will fix it at no charge. If we cannot fix it, we refund that page.
  • Page count adjustments: if your files contain fewer pages than you paid for, we refund the difference. If they contain more, we invoice the extra pages at the same rate before starting them.
  • Rush service: if we miss a rush deadline, we refund the rush fee.
  • Refunds go back to the original payment method, usually within 5 to 10 business days.

Terms of service

Services

ClearPath Remediation makes PDF and other electronic documents accessible and tests web content against Section 508 and WCAG 2.1 Level AA. Per-page prices and turnaround times are listed on our pricing section. Turnaround begins when we have received both payment (or a purchase order) and all files.

What counts as a page

One page is one PDF page as numbered in the file, regardless of how much content it holds. A cover, blank page, or appendix page each counts as one page.

Your files

You confirm you have the right to share the files you send us. You keep all rights to your content. We do not change the meaning of your content; we change only its structure, tags, and accessibility properties. Typos or errors in the source document are your responsibility, though we will flag any we notice.

Conformance

We remediate to WCAG 2.1 Level AA and the PDF/UA standard and test with industry tools and a screen reader. Accessibility conformance is a technical assessment, not legal advice, and we cannot guarantee any legal outcome.

Government purchasing

We accept purchase orders and can invoice net 30. A W-9 is available on request.

Liability

Our total liability for any order is limited to the amount you paid for that order.

Privacy policy

  • This website uses no cookies, analytics, or tracking scripts.
  • Payments are processed by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number. See Stripe's privacy policy.
  • Your documents are used only to complete your order. We do not share them with anyone. We delete them 30 days after delivery unless you ask us to keep them longer.
  • Sensitive content: if your files contain personal or confidential information, tell us before sending and we will agree on a secure transfer method.
  • Contact details you give us (name, email, organization) are used only to deliver your order and communicate with you.
